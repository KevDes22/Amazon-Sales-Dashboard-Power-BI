# Amazon Sales Dashboard — Power BI

> **Role:** Business Analyst | **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI | **Pages:** 3 (Objectives · Diagnostics · Descriptive)

---

## Introduction

This project presents an end-to-end sales analytics dashboard built in Power BI for an Amazon retail dataset. The dashboard spans three analytical pages — Objectives, Diagnostics, and Descriptive — and is designed to give stakeholders a clear, interactive view of revenue performance, customer behaviour, logistics, and seller activity across multiple markets.

---

## Problem Statement

The business lacked a centralised view of its sales operations, making it difficult to:
- Track revenue trends across brands, categories, and geographies
- Identify fulfillment bottlenecks driving order cancellations and returns
- Evaluate seller performance and payment method distribution
- Understand which customer segments and product categories drive the most value

## Key Findings

- **Total Revenue:** ₹91.83M across the analysed period, with **100K+ orders** recorded
- **Top Brands:** CoreTech, KidsMate, and ReadMore led revenue generation; HomeEase and BrightLux trailed
- **Revenue by Category:** Electronics and Sports & Outdoors were the highest-grossing categories; Clothing and Home & Kitchen underperformed relatively
- **Order Status:** A significant volume of orders were delivered or shipped, but a notable share were marked as **Pending, Returned, or Cancelled** — signalling fulfilment and satisfaction gaps
- **Payment Methods:** Credit Card and Debit Card dominated; Cash on Delivery and Net Banking had lower adoption
- **Shipping Cost:** Average shipping cost was consistent across countries (~₹7.33–₹7.46), suggesting a flat-rate or standardised logistics model
- **Geographic Revenue:** The United States and India drove the bulk of total revenue; Australia and Canada lagged
- **Seller Performance:** Top sellers (e.g., SELL00006, SELL01099) each moved 230+ units; the top 10 sellers collectively accounted for 2,208 units
- **Seasonality:** Revenue by month showed a notable mid-year dip with recovery toward year-end (October–December)

---

## Recommendations

1. **Reduce Return & Cancellation Rates** — Investigate root causes (quality, delivery delays, mislabelling) for the pending and returned order segments; implement proactive customer communication workflows
2. **Double Down on High-Revenue Categories** — Prioritise inventory and marketing spend on Electronics and Sports & Outdoors; assess profitability of underperforming categories before scaling
3. **Incentivise Digital Payments** — Drive adoption of UPI and Amazon Pay through targeted promotions to reduce Cash on Delivery operational costs
4. **Develop Underperforming Markets** — Australia and Canada show low revenue relative to other regions; localised campaigns or seller recruitment could unlock growth
5. **Leverage Top Seller Insights** — Identify what top-performing sellers do differently (pricing, fulfilment speed, product mix) and replicate those practices across the seller base
6. **Address Mid-Year Revenue Dip** — Introduce targeted promotions or sales events in June–August to smooth seasonal revenue troughs

## Conclusion

This dashboard demonstrates the power of visual analytics in transforming raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence. By consolidating revenue, logistics, seller, and customer data into a single Power BI report, stakeholders can make faster, evidence-based decisions. The insights highlight clear opportunities to improve fulfilment efficiency, optimise category focus, and expand market reach — all critical levers for sustainable revenue growth.
